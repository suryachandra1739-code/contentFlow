ContentFlow Client Portal: User Manual

Welcome to the ContentFlow Client Portal! This manual is designed to guide you through all the features available in your dashboard, ensuring a seamless experience for reviewing, approving, and tracking your social media content.

1. Overview Dashboard

The Overview page (/client-portal) is your central hub for managing your content approval workflow.

Key Features:

  • Personalized Greeting: Displays your name and provides a welcoming entry point.
  • Performance & Status Statistics: At a glance, view four key metrics:
    • Total posts submitted: The lifetime count of all posts submitted to your account.
    • Awaiting your review: The number of posts that currently need your attention and approval.
    • Approved by you: The total number of posts you have successfully approved for publishing.
    • Revision requested: Posts you have sent back to the team for edits.
  • Pending Review Queue: A streamlined list of all posts that are awaiting your decision. Each item displays:
    • A media thumbnail (image or video preview).
    • The post's title and a snippet of the caption.
    • The target platform (e.g., LinkedIn, Instagram), project name, and submission date.
    • A direct Review button to jump straight to the approval interface.

2. Post Review Interface

Clicking "Review" on any post takes you to the dedicated Post Review page, where you can inspect the content in detail and make decisions.

Key Features:

  • Interactive Platform Preview: See exactly how the post will look once published on the specific target platform (e.g., an Instagram feed or a LinkedIn post format).
  • Full Caption Text: Read the complete caption that will accompany your media.
  • Decision Center: The core area where you take action on a post:
    • Approve Post: If everything looks perfect, click here to approve the post instantly.
    • Request Changes: If edits are needed, click this button to open a feedback form. You must provide specific details on what needs to be changed before submitting the request.
  • AI-Assisted Optimization Insights: Premium metrics to help you understand the potential impact of your post:
    • Expected Reach: A projected estimate of how many users the post will reach.
    • Engagement Index: A score indicating how well the post aligns with platform best practices.
    • Optimization Suggestions: Actionable recommendations (like adding tags or changing timing) along with the estimated percentage boost they could provide.
  • Real-time Feedback Feed: A chronological, real-time chat and log of all comments and requests related to the post. You can see feedback from team members, admins, and your own previous comments, categorized by user roles.

3. History Page

The History tab (/client-portal/history) provides a complete archive of every post associated with your account.

Key Features:

  • Comprehensive Table View: Displays all posts with columns for Media, Caption, Platform, Status, and Date.
  • Advanced Filtering: Easily filter your history by post status:
    • All statuses
    • Approved
    • Pending
    • Revision
    • Rejected
  • Sorting Capabilities: Sort your posts chronologically by "Newest first" or "Oldest first".
  • Quick Access: Click the "View" button on any historical post to see its original review page, comments, and metrics.

4. Activity Log

The Activity tab (/client-portal/activity) acts as a detailed audit trail for your account.

Key Features:

  • Chronological Timeline: View every single action taken regarding your content, from newest to oldest.
  • Tracked Actions: The system automatically logs:
    • When new posts are submitted for review.
    • When posts are approved.
    • When revisions are requested (including the specific feedback provided).
    • When posts are rejected.
    • When new comments are added.
  • Detailed Context: Each entry shows exactly who performed the action (e.g., which team member or if it was you), the exact timestamp, and any associated comment text.

5. Global Navigation & Notifications

  • Persistent Header: No matter where you are in the portal, the top header remains accessible.
  • Notification Bell: Located in the top right, the notification bell alerts you to important updates, such as newly submitted posts or replies to your revision requests.
  • Account Information: Your company name and personal name are always visible.
  • Log Out: Securely sign out of your session using the "Log out" button in the header.

If you have any further questions or encounter any issues, please contact your account manager.
